Delete Your Account — Profit Keeper Mentor

Last updated: October 2026

If you would like to delete your Profit Keeper Mentor account and associated data, follow the steps below.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Open the Profit Keeper Mentor app and go to the Support Chat, or contact us directly on WhatsApp.
  2. Send a message requesting account deletion, including the email address registered on your account.
  3. Our team will verify your request and delete your account within 7 business days.

Alternatively, you can email your deletion request to ukashafarooqi05@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and your registered email address.

What Data Is Deleted

  • Your account credentials (email, password)
  • Your profile information (name, phone number)
  • Your course access records
  • Your chat messages
  • Your device identifier

What Data Is Kept

For legal and accounting purposes, records of course enrollment history may be retained in anonymized form for up to 1 year after account deletion, as required for business record-keeping. This data cannot be used to identify you after deletion.

Timeframe

Account deletion requests are processed within 7 business days of verification.
