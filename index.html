Privacy Policy — Profit Keeper

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Profit Keeper ("the App") collects, uses, and protects your information. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, email address, and phone number provided during sign-up.
  • Device identifier: a unique device ID used to prevent account sharing between multiple devices.
  • Push notification token: used to deliver notifications about your course access, chat messages, and account status.
  • Chat messages: messages you send to the admin/support team within the App.
  • Course access records: which courses you have been granted access to, and for how long.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To grant and manage access to purchased course content.
  • To send you notifications relevant to your account and course access.
  • To provide customer support via in-app chat or WhatsApp.
  • To prevent unauthorized account sharing.

Payments

Profit Keeper Mentor does not process payments or collect payment card or banking credentials within the app. Access to courses is managed by the app administrator. Users may contact the administrator for support or information about course access.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Your data is stored securely with our backend provider (Supabase) and is only accessible to authorized administrators of the App. We do not share your data with advertisers or third-party marketing services.

Data Security

We use industry-standard security practices, including encrypted connections and access controls, to protect your information. Course video content is protected against unauthorized screen recording where technically supported by your device.

Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you request account deletion, your account will be deactivated and your personal data handled according to applicable law.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting the course administrator through the App's support chat or WhatsApp.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the Profit Keeper admin team via the in-app chat or WhatsApp.
